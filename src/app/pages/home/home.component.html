METROLOGY & INTEGRITY

QUALITY & TECHNOLOGY

Precision textile performance is validated through disciplined metrology. Our in-house physical testing lab monitors fiber micronaire, USTER imperfection indices, and 4-point ASTM defect grading across all batches.

01

High Volume Instrument (HVI) Testing

Scientific analysis of fiber length, strength, elongation, and trash content before bale processing.

02

Online USTER Imperfection Monitoring

Continuous yarn clearing eliminating foreign matter, thin/thick places, and neps for smooth weaving.

03

ASTM D5430 4-Point Greige Grading

Full fabric width inspection on backlit inspection frames for export-grade selvage and pick uniformity.
