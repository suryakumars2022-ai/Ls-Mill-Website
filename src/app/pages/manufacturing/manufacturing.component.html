Spinning & Compact Yarn Division

Our spinning floors convert tested cotton bales into high-tenacity, low-hairiness yarns. Equipped with machinery from world leaders LMW, Rieter, Muratec, and SSM, our facility commands 150,000 spindles producing 25 tonnes daily of ultra-uniform yarns.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITY PARAMETERS:

  • Count Spectrum: Ne 40s to Ne 200s Count SIRO Compact Slub Yarn & Plied varieties.
  • Plied & Gassed Yarn: Ne 40/2 to Ne 200/2 Gassed Yarn supplied in hard and soft dye cone packages.
  • Raw Fibers: Egyptian Giza, Supima, BCI, and 100% GOTS Certified Organic Cotton.
  • Quality Clearing: Real-time Uster testing and optical clearers eliminating 100% of foreign fibers.

Weaving & Fabric Architecture

Engineered warp beams enter climate-controlled weaving sheds equipped with Benninger warping/sizing lines and high-speed Tsudakoma airjet looms. We manufacture flawless grey fabrics exported widely to Europe and the United States.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITY PARAMETERS:

  • Thread Count Spectrum: 200 Thread Count up to ultra-luxury 1,500 Thread Count.
  • Weaving Width: Up to 340 cm wide greige fabric for seamless king and super-king bedding.
  • Weave Architectures: Satin, Percale, Twill, Dobby, Seersucker, Waffle, Oxford, and Stripes.
  • Fibers Utilized: Supima, Egyptian, BCI, Organic Cotton, Tencel, Refibra, Linen, Modal, and Hemp.
