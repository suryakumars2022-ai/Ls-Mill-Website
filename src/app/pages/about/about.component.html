FOUNDATION & EVOLUTION

From Regional Spinning Pioneer to Integrated Global Manufacturer

Established in 1979 in Theni, Tamil Nadu under the stewardship of Managing Director Mr. S. Manivannan, LS Mills Limited began with a focused commitment: to harness South India’s rich cotton belt through uncompromising machine discipline.

Over forty-five years of deliberate engineering investment, LS Mills Limited expanded to 150,000 spindles spinning 25 tonnes daily of premium compact, combed, and SIRO slub yarns. Our spinning halls operate premier international machinery from LMW, Rieter, Muratec, and SSM, producing counts from Ne 40s to ultra-fine Ne 200s.

Responding to international luxury retail demand, we integrated wide-width Tsudakoma airjet looms and Benninger sizing systems weaving grey fabrics up to 1,500 thread counts. Today, our dedicated cut-and-sew units yield 6,000 luxury sheets per shift, represented worldwide through our permanent commercial showroom at 230 Fifth Avenue, New York.
