01 / DIVISION BLOWROOM • COMBING • RING SPINNING
FIBER TO YARN

Spinning & Compact Yarn Division

Our spinning floors convert tested cotton bales into high-tenacity, low-hairiness yarns. Automated blowrooms systematically open, blend, and clean cotton staples, followed by precision carding and multi-pass drawframes that align fibers into uniform slivers.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITY PARAMETERS:

  • Count Spectrum: Ne 20/1 through Ne 100/1 in combed ring spun and compact configurations.
  • Combing Efficiency: Short fiber removal tuned between 14% and 22% for optimal yarn strength.
  • Electronic Clearing: Automatic optical yarn clearing to eradicate foreign contamination and thick/thin defects.

02 / DIVISION WARP SIZING • AIRJET LOOMS • DOBBY
YARN TO FABRIC

Weaving & Fabric Architecture

Engineered warp beams enter climate-controlled weaving sheds equipped with high-speed airjet and rapier looms. Continuous electronic weft insertion ensures uniform pick density and flawless selvage formation.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITY PARAMETERS:

  • Weaving Width: Up to 340 cm wide greige fabric for seamless king and super-king bedding.
  • Density Range: 180 Thread Count up to 1000 Thread Count in percales, sateens, and stripes.
  • Weave Geometry: Plain 1/1, Sateen 4/1, Twill 2/1, 3/1, and complex Dobby geometric designs.

Finished Home Textiles

Transforming master fabric into export-grade bed ensembles. Automated cutting tables ensure squared dimensions, while skilled tailoring lines execute mitered corners, double needle top-stitching, and customized retail banderoles.
